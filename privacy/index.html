Privacy

This covers the Free AI Audit form on teamwideai.com (it replaced the AI Map form). Updated November 16, 2026.

What we collect

  • Your first name, email, company, and, if you add them, your phone and website.
  • Your answers: how much your company makes in a year, your team size, your role, what would break if you left for 30 days, and when you want to start.
  • That you agreed we can email or text you about your audit.
  • Where you came from (like Instagram) and how much of the video you watched.

Why

To prepare your audit, to set up and run your call, and to follow up with you about it. If you book a time, the booking tool also gets your name and email.

Page counts

The audit page counts steps like a visit, a play of the video, or a step of the form, and how long and how far: seconds on the page and of the video, how far down you scrolled, which question you reached. That is so we know what works. Each count carries a random number that lasts one page load, where you came from, the kind of device (phone or computer) and your time zone, and nothing else. No cookies, and nothing that says who you are.

What we never do

We never sell your info. We don’t rent it or share it for anyone else’s ads.

Stop or delete

Reply stop to any text, or tell us by email, and we stop. To see or delete what we have on you, email blue@sellbychat.ai and we’ll do it.
