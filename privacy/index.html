Privacy

This covers the AI Map form on teamwideai.com. Updated November 13, 2026.

What we collect

  • Your first name, email, company, and, if you add them, your phone and website.
  • Your answers: how much your company makes in a year, your team size, and what eats your week.
  • That you agreed we can email or text you about your AI Map.

Why

To build your AI Map, to set up and run your call, and to follow up with you about it. If you book a time, the booking tool also gets your name and email.

What we never do

We never sell your info. We don’t rent it or share it for anyone else’s ads.

Stop or delete

Reply stop to any text, or tell us by email, and we stop. To see or delete what we have on you, email blue@sellbychat.ai and we’ll do it.
