Overdue invoice reminder: the 3 messages that chase who owes you

Copy this setup into Claude or ChatGPT with your list of unpaid invoices. You read every message before it goes out.

By Blue WalkerNovember 20, 2026Updated November 24, 2026

What you need

A list of your unpaid invoices: customer, contact first name, invoice number, what the work was, amount, and days past due. QuickBooks, Jobber and Housecall Pro can all export this. If you’re on QuickBooks, Claude already has a ready “chase invoices” workflow built in, so start there.

The rules (paste these first)

You write invoice reminders for my business. I am the owner. Write as me, first person, plain and polite, like I am texting a customer I want to keep.

My rules:
Day 7: friendly reminder with the invoice.
Day 21: firm, with the payment link.
Day 35: stop and tell me.
Never threaten.

Use the days past due to pick the stage:
7 to 20 days: friendly.
21 to 34 days: firm.
35 days or more: do not write to the customer. Write me one line saying who to call, the amount, how late, and why a call is the right move.

Every customer message:
Starts with "Hi <first name>,".
Names the actual work, the invoice number and the exact amount.
Says how many days late only in the firm stage.
Ends with my name and my business name.
Under 320 characters, text message length.

Never:
Threaten anything: no collections, lawyers, court, credit, stopping service, "final notice".
Add or mention fees, interest or penalties.
Guilt the customer or bring up their payment history.
Make up facts, dates or discounts.

Write one result per invoice. I read every one before anything goes out.

What comes back

The names and numbers below are a made up example business.

Message 1: Day 7, friendly

Hi Priya, a quick reminder about invoice 2327 for $640 for your fall cleanup and bulb planting. I've attached it for you. Thanks! Dana, Copperleaf Landscaping

Message 2: Day 21, firm

Hi Tom, invoice 2305 for $4,220, for September maintenance and the parking lot island cleanup, is now 27 days past due. Could you pay this week at [pay link]? If anything on the invoice looks wrong, tell me and I'll fix it. Dana, Copperleaf Landscaping

Message 3: Day 35, the note to you (nothing goes to the customer)

Call Darnell at Pinegate Properties, $2,940, 41 days late. He says Pine Lake wasn't finished, but crew notes say it was done Aug 28.

How to run it every Monday

  1. Export your unpaid invoices.
  2. Paste the rules, then the list.
  3. Read every message. Fix anything that sounds wrong.
  4. Send the friendly and firm ones from your own email or texting tool, with the invoice and your real payment link.
  5. Call the Day 35 ones yourself. That is the only part a person should do.

Only text customers who agreed to get texts from you, and stop when someone says STOP.
