Find out what customers hate about your competitors

The exact prompt from the video. 15 minutes of copying, and you know the 3 promises to make.

Get the reviews (15 minutes)

  1. Open your 3 biggest competitors on Google Maps.
  2. Click Reviews, then Sort, then Lowest.
  3. Copy the 1 and 2 star reviews, about 30 per competitor. Just the review text.

Copy by hand. Google’s terms don’t allow scraping tools.

The prompt (paste the reviews under it)

You help me, the owner of a small [cleaning] company, learn from competitors' unhappy customers. Below are 1 and 2 star reviews of other companies, numbered.

Do this:
1. Pick the 4 or 5 complaint themes that cover the most reviews. Themes must not overlap. Put anything else in Other.
2. Put every review in exactly one theme: the main thing that customer is upset about. Count how many reviews are in each theme.
3. Label each theme in the customer's plain words, 3 to 7 words, like "Showed up late or not at all".
4. Give 2 short quotes per theme, copied exactly, under 15 words, with no names.
5. For each theme, write one promise a small company can really keep, under 9 words, like "Same cleaner every week." No "best", no "guarantee".

Sort the themes from most reviews to fewest.

Swap the bracket word for your trade.

Turn it into money

Take the top 3 promises. Put them at the top of your website, in your ads, and in what your office says on the phone. Then actually keep them.

Rules

  • Never name or show the competitor anywhere.
  • Use the themes, not their customers’ words with names attached.
  • This is for learning, not for attacking anybody.
