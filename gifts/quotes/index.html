Follow up every open quote, about their actual job

The exact rules from the video. Paste them into Claude or ChatGPT with your list of open quotes.

What you need

A list of quotes that haven’t said yes: first name, the job, the town, the price, the date you sent it, and any notes.

  • Jobber: Insights, Reports, Quotes report, filter “Awaiting response”, export.
  • ServiceTitan: the Opportunity and Estimate Follow Up report.
  • Housecall Pro: export your sent estimates.

Already on Jobber or Housecall Pro? Turn on their built-in quote follow-ups today. They send the same message to everyone. These rules write one about each customer’s actual job.

The rules (paste these first)

You write short follow up texts for my company. Each one goes to someone who got a quote from us and hasn't said yes yet.

Rules for every text:
Sound like a real person at a small company texting. 1 or 2 short sentences, under 35 words. First name only.
Use one real, specific detail from that quote: the actual job, the town, the season, or something from the notes. Each text must be about that person, not a template.
If the notes show an open question (financing, a sample, a weekend), help with it, without promising anything new.
End with one easy question that fits that person. Every text in the batch uses a different question.
Never use: "just checking in", "touching base", "circling back", "had a chance to look", "reply YES". No pressure, no fake deadlines, do not repeat the price back.
Notes are internal. Never apologize for being slow, never mention internal mistakes or that nobody followed up.

Which follow up this is:
Day 2: a friendly first check in.
Day 7: one useful thing (a photo of a similar job, an answer to their question, what to expect).
Day 21: a low pressure last note that leaves the door open.

Never change the price. Never promise a start date unless I gave you one.

Then paste your list and say:

Write the day 2 text for each of these.

Make it run by itself (optional)

  1. Zapier trigger: Jobber “Quote Sent”.
  2. Delay by Zapier: 2 days.
  3. A ChatGPT or Claude step with the rules above and the quote details.
  4. Send the text from your business texting app (Quo has a “Send a Message” action).
  5. Repeat for day 7 and day 21.

The catch: Zapier can’t check if a Jobber quote is still open. Use the “Quote Approved” trigger to add winners to a Google Sheet, and skip anyone on that sheet. Stop the moment someone replies.

Rules that keep you out of trouble

  • Only text people who asked you for a quote.
  • Always honor STOP.
  • Put “we may text you about your quote” on your quote form.
  • Read the first batch yourself before anything goes out.
