Missed call text back, plus the emergency rule

The exact setup from the video. Every missed call gets a text, and AI flags the emergencies.

Step 1: turn on the text back (5 minutes)

Your phone app probably has it already:

  • Quo (OpenPhone): Settings, Phone Numbers, your number, Automations, Auto-Replies. Turn on missed calls.
  • Grasshopper: Instant Response.
  • Jobber Receptionist: “SMS send back” texts callers who hang up.
  • Housecall Pro: Settings, Inbox, Voice, missed call automations.
  • RingCentral: the Automator “Missed call auto-response” template. Calls that go to voicemail don’t trigger it, so set that up too.

The text:

Hey, it's [your company]. We're on a job and missed you. What's going on, and what's the address?

Keep it that short. No promises, no times, no prices.

Step 2: the smart part, AI reads the reply

With Quo and Zapier:

  1. Trigger: Quo “Incoming Message Received”.
  2. A ChatGPT or Claude step with the rule below.
  3. If it says URGENT, text your own cell with the summary.
  4. Everything else goes on one Google Sheet for when you’re free.

The rule (paste this into the AI step):

You read a customer's text to a [plumbing] company. Decide if it is an emergency.

Emergency: [a burst pipe, flooding, water coming through a ceiling, a sewer backup, no hot water in winter, no heat, a gas smell].
For a gas smell, also tell them: leave the house and call the gas company or 911 from outside.

Return one line only:
URGENT: <what is wrong in 5 words>, <the address if they gave one>
or
NORMAL: <what they need in 5 words>

Never diagnose. Never give a price or a time.

Swap the bracket words for your trade.

What it costs

Quo is about $15 to $35 a user a month. Twilio, if you build it yourself, is about $1.15 a month for the number and about a cent a text.

Rules

  • Texting from your own US number needs carrier registration (10DLC). Plan on 1 to 2 weeks. The phone apps above handle it for you.
  • Only text people who called you.
  • Always honor STOP.
