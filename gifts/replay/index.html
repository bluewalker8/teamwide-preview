The rule that finds every call you should have booked

The exact rule from the video. AI reads every recorded call and shows you the line where the booking slipped.

The rule

Paste this into Claude or ChatGPT with a call transcript.

You review recorded phone calls for my [HVAC] company. Follow my rule exactly.

My rule:
Flag any call where the caller had a problem we fix and did not get a booked time. Flag [no-heat] calls with kids or elderly people as urgent. Quote the exact line where it slipped.

For each call, tell me:
Problem: the caller's problem in a few plain words, and who is at risk if anyone.
Bookable: yes if the caller had a problem we fix.
Booked: yes only if they got a booked time (a day and time, or a tech on the way).
Urgent: yes for [no heat] with kids or elderly people.
Where it slipped: the exact words that lost the booking, copied from the transcript.
What to do: one plain sentence a busy owner reads in 3 seconds.

Swap the bracket words for your trade.

How to run it every week

  1. Record your calls. Quo, CallRail and most phone systems do it. Play a “this call may be recorded” notice.
  2. Get the text of each call. Quo Business makes transcripts for you. If yours doesn’t, Deepgram turns a recording into text for about 2 cents a call.
  3. Zapier: Quo “Call Transcript Completed” (add a 5 minute delay, transcripts lag), then the AI step with the rule, then a row in a Google Sheet.
  4. Every Monday, sort the sheet by “Bookable yes, Booked no”. That’s your list.

On ServiceTitan? Its Voice Intelligence already flags bookable calls that didn’t book. Ask your account manager to turn it on.

Use it right

It’s to fix the script, not to yell at anybody. Read the flagged lines together once a week. Write the better line on a sticky note by the phone.

Rules

About 11 to 13 US states need everyone on the call to agree to recording, including California, Florida, Illinois, Pennsylvania and Washington. Play the notice at the start of every call and you’re covered.
