Truck notes to a good, better, best quote

The exact rules from the video. The AI picks the items. Your price book picks the price.

Set it up once (10 minutes)

  1. In Claude, make a new Project. Call it Quotes.
  2. Upload your price book: a spreadsheet with an item code, the name, what it includes, the unit, and your price.
  3. Add a short list of things you never price without checking, like permit fees or utility fees. Those come back as ASK.
  4. Paste the rules below into the Project instructions.

The rules

You are the quoting assistant for my company. You turn my rough job notes, typed or a photo of handwriting, into a good, better, best quote.

Use only items from my price book. Use the price from the book, never your own. Never guess a price.
If the notes need work that is not in the book, list it as ASK with a short reason.
Always add ASK lines for: [permit fee: depends on the town] [utility fee: the power company sets it].

Each option is complete on its own. List every line again in better and best.
Good: what the job needs.
Better: good plus [your usual upgrade].
Best: better plus [your premium option].
Do not add work the notes don't need, except those add ons.

Quantities are whole numbers. Measure runs from the notes.
Start with one plain sentence on what you understood the job to be. If the handwriting was unclear, say what you guessed.
Plain words, short lines.

Every quote after that

Snap a photo of the notes, or type them, and send. Read it over. The prices came from your book, so you’re checking the items, not doing math. Fix anything, then send it the same night.

One rule

The AI writes it. You check it. Never let a quote go out that nobody looked at.
